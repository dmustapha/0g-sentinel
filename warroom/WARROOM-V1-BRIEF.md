- **Round 3.5:** Premortem for top 3 — "We submitted and LOST. Why?"

### FINAL SCORING FORMULA

```
FINAL = (Norm_Vote × 0.30) + (Weighted_Criteria_Avg × 0.50) + (Norm_YC_PQ × 0.20) + Bonuses
```
Where:
- Norm_Vote = (vote points / 12) × 10
- Weighted_Criteria_Avg = sum(criterion_score × weight)
- Norm_YC_PQ = (YC PQ score / 6) × 10
- Bonuses: Competition check (+1.0) + Demo-product gap (+1.0)

**YC PQ < 3 = eliminated before scoring.**
