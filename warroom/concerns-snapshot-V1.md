# Concerns Snapshot — V1
**Hackathon:** 0G APAC Hackathon
**Date:** 2026-05-13

---

## CRITICAL CONCERNS (Idea eliminated if violated)

1. **[C] Time NOT a constraint.** Claude Code = 10x dev speed. Do NOT penalize ideas for complexity. Ambitious projects are achievable.
2. **[C] Uniqueness is non-negotiable.** Zero competitors in the target space is strongly preferred. Verify competition for EACH idea explicitly.
3. **[C] "Does this help real humans?" test.** Every idea must pass it. If you can't point to real people whose lives improve, the idea fails.
4. **[C] Cumulative corrections (nothing dropped).** Every correction from every version carries forward. Nothing is silently dropped.
5. **[C] Must solve a SIGNIFICANT, real problem.** "Would I still build this if there were no prize?" is the test.
6. **[C] Must serve actual target users who exist TODAY.** Identifiable, reachable people — not future users after ecosystem matures.

---

## IMPORTANT CONCERNS (Score penalty if violated)

7. **[I] Everything is devnet/testnet.** Mocks are fine. Judge on concept and demo, not production infrastructure.
8. **[I] Read ALL research data.** Use everything — Discord intel, web research, competitor analysis, winning patterns.
9. **[I] Take your time, be extensive.** 200-400 words per proposal, specific citations, 800+ lines of genuine debate.
10. **[I] Focused product, BROAD problem.** Niche scope is fine. Niche audience is NOT. Problem affects millions.
11. **[I] Winning AND real impact are not mutually exclusive.** Find ideas where both align.
12. **[I] Demo must feel like the real product.** Judges should see a product, not a prototype.

---

## ADVISORY CONCERNS (Noted but not disqualifying)

13. **[A] Fresh ideas allowed.** Not limited to any list. Agents can invent new ideas.
14. **[A] Reframing is on the table.** Find the best problem-technology fit.

---

## CONTEXTUAL CONCERNS (Selected for this hackathon)

15. **[A] AI/Agents should be considered when appropriate.** This IS an AI-first hackathon — all 5 tracks involve AI agents as primary feature, all judging criteria are evaluated through the AI agent lens. This does NOT mean every idea must be agent-based; it means AI agent architectures are appropriate and expected here.

16. **[I] Confidential compute / privacy angle.** Track 5 (Privacy & Sovereign Infrastructure) explicitly focuses on TEE and ZK. 0G Private Computer is live. Privacy-preserving AI is underbuilt relative to demand. Ideas targeting T5 or integrating TEE get bonus evaluation weight on the integration depth criterion.
